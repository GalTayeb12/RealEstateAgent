# Art direction

Quality over quantity. Procedural/parametric assets must pass the same bar as modeled ones. Put reference images in `references/`.

| Style | Palette and materials | Form language | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Modern Minimalist | white, greige, matte lacquer, pale oak | low, linear, hidden hardware | ornament |
| Japandi | light oak/ash, linen, paper, charcoal | low profiles, soft rectangles | glossy finishes |
| Organic Modern | boucle, travertine, limewash plaster, ceramic | curved, sculptural, rounded | hard edges everywhere |
| Contemporary | mixed metal + stone, tailored upholstery | clean with one statement piece | trend overload |
| Quiet Luxury | walnut, marble, wool, brushed brass | deep, generous, restrained | logos, shine |
| Scandinavian | birch/white oak, wool, soft pastels | light, airy, tapered legs | heavy dark tones |
| Modern Industrial | blackened steel, concrete, microcement, leather | frames, grids | rust cliches |

Hero priorities: sculptural/modular sofas, travertine and marble tables, upholstered chairs, upholstered beds, designer pendants/floor lamps, fluted wood panels, microcement, natural stone, premium cabinet fronts, curated accessories.
Forbidden: box furniture, cartoon/dated styling, faceted curves, stretched textures, missing bevels, flat single-colour upholstery.
