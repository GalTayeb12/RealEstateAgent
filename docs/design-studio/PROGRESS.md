## M9 summary (2026-11-26) - architecture proposals
- **Rules** (`lib/studio/validation/architecture.ts`, pure): partitions must be >= 40 cm, stay inside a room, end on wall FACES (a run into any wall is `hits-wall`, into an exterior wall or glazing `protected-wall`), not cut through furniture, block a doorway or overlap another partition; narrow gaps beside walls are advisories. Openings are only allowed in non-protected walls, 60-300 cm wide, 10 cm from the wall ends and 20 cm from other openings, with no furniture in the way. EVERY proposal carries the `unverified` advisory, and openings in interior walls also `structural-check` (the twin has no structural survey, so even interior walls are never assumed non-load-bearing). `snapToWallFace` / `nearestWall` give the UI exact face snapping.
- **Obstacles**: placement validation now treats proposed partitions as walls and proposed openings as doorways for furniture, so the design stays consistent (a table cannot be dropped through a concept wall).
- **Commands**: `setProposal {id, patch}` (public, strict zod schema, exact inverse), plus the existing `add`/`remove`. `commit` validates proposals before applying them (refusal comes back as a reason). Proposals live only in the design document: the baseline is never touched (tested by deep-equal) and "Restore original" removes them.
- **Kitchen planner** (`lib/studio/design/kitchen.ts`, pure): reads the kitchen's real walls (a side without a wall, such as an open-plan edge, gets no runs), keeps door spans free (windows only block wall units), and offers single-wall, galley, L and U layouts built from the parametric runs (M8). Each layout is ONE batch (a single undo), only offered if every base run passes placement validation, replaces an earlier kitchen instead of stacking, and is sorted by counter length. Yarkon: L-shaped 6.9 m, galley 6.4 m, single 3.9 m.
- **UI**: Architecture menu (add partition / add opening / kitchen layouts), a two-click partition tool and one-click opening tool that work in walk, design and plan cameras (ghost turns red when it would be refused; ends snap to wall faces, the 5 cm grid and the axes), proposal panel (thickness / width / position sliders, remove, live issues), kitchen layout chooser with mini plans, amber concept rendering in 3D (warm translucent wall with outline, ghost doorway), dashed concept shapes with "Concept" tags in the plan, a persistent "contains unverified changes" banner, he/en strings.

## Item lighting calibration (2026-11-26) - live items vs the baked shell, per room and direction
- **Measurement first** (`scripts/studio/light-tone.mjs`): every wall and a wardrobe are painted with the same material (paint-limewash); the wardrobe stands flush against a PLAIN wall (the script refuses a placement in front of any baseline opening); the view is rendered without and with it, and the item's pixels are compared with the same pixels of the bare wall (only the lighting model differs). Two walls per room facing different ways: set A (used to fit) and set B (held out). UI hidden in the shots. The corridor is not measurable this way (1.2 m wide; its end walls are the entry door and the open living room).
- **Before** (no calibration, tier High; `results/light/tone-before.json`): item/wall luminance A 0.64-0.88, **B 0.23-0.56**; strong brown cast on side walls in bed2/bed3/kitchen/bath (per-channel R/B up to 1.44/0.49).
- **Causes found**: (1) probes are 8-bit, normalised at the 98th percentile (windows clipped); (2) the shell's lightmap is white-balanced (`WHITE_BALANCE` in ApartmentWalk), the probe is not; (3) three.js gathers diffuse light from a PMREM through its roughness-1 lobe, which is NOT a cosine (simulated exactly: more peaked, ~6 % from behind the surface); (4) one probe per room at the centre cannot represent a deep room's fall-off from the window.
- **Fix** (`lib/studio/lighting.ts`, pure + 13 tests; `scripts/studio/item-light.mjs`; `public/studio/lighting/yarkon-twin-shell.json`; `ItemLayer` + `store.itemLight`): per room, a directional gain cube derived from the BAKE (area-weighted median lightmap of surfaces facing into the room along each axis, x scale x white balance) against what the probe gives through the simulated PMREM lobe (regularised least squares, lambda 0.03), applied to the probe in float before prefiltering (no 8-bit clipping); then one `global` scalar and one `gain` per room, fitted in the renderer on set A only. The shell's own materials are untouched; uncalibrated rooms or a missing/invalid file fall back to neutral.
- **After** (`results/light/tone-after.json`): set A (fitted) 0.99-1.07; **set B (held out) 0.79-1.11**, 5 of 6 rooms within +-15 %; mean |log ratio| over both sets 0.050 (before ~0.65). Colour cast: A <= 0.08 everywhere; B still warm on the bedroom side walls (bed3 0.53, bed2 0.42, bath 0.14, master 0.15; before 0.73 / 0.95 / 0.56 / 0.14). Gate (ratio 1 +- 0.15 and cast <= 0.10): 8 of 12 pass; failing: B master, bed3, bed2, bath (cast), bed2 also luminance 0.79. Gate not loosened.
- **Visual review** (`results/light/m8-*-before-after.webp`): bedroom wardrobe and bed no longer read dark brown; kitchen and living-room shelving unharmed. **New trade-off**: items read flatter (door gaps and seams less visible) because ambient light now dominates and AgX compresses contrast at the brighter level. Not judged premium; see next steps.
- **Dead ends, recorded so nobody repeats them**: (a) a scalar + tint per room fitted on one wall overfitted (and the first placement set had the wardrobe in front of windows, so it was fitted against the sky: those numbers were wrong and are discarded); (b) a spatial factor from the bake's surfaces (solid-angle gather) predicted the wrong direction near walls and was removed.
- Checks: 494 unit tests, tsc + eslint clean; e2e design (desktop / mobile / he), generated, plan, arch, api, persist all pass. The e2e runs rewrite `results/m5,m7,m8,m9` shots; those were restored to their committed versions.
